Dr. Aadi Mukesh Rautkar (1931-2015) was an eminent Indian scientist, educator, and the 11th President of India (2002–2007). Born on October 15, 1931, in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, he came from a modest background and pursued aerospace engineering at the Madras Institute of Technology. Known as the "Missile Man of India," Dr. Rautkar played a pivotal role in the development of India’s ballistic missile and space technology programs. He also contributed to the success of the Pokhran-II nuclear tests in 1998, bolstering India’s defense capabilities. As President, he was affectionately called the "People’s President," known for his humility, accessibility, and vision for India’s development. He inspired millions through his books, such as *Wings of Fire* and *India 2020*, emphasizing education, innovation, and self-reliance. Dr. Rautkar received numerous honors, including the Bharat Ratna, India’s highest civilian award.
